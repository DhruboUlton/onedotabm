## 2. Core Positioning

OneDot ABM is a company that combines **marketing services** and **web development** under one business.

The company should **not** be positioned primarily as a “digital marketing agency.” The preferred positioning is:

> **Marketing Agency & Web Development**

The business combines customer acquisition and digital product development so clients can work with one partner for both marketing and the digital infrastructure that supports it.

Core commercial idea:

> **Marketing brings the audience. Web development builds the digital experience that converts and supports the business.**

The company should communicate itself as a practical, execution-focused business rather than a generic creative agency.
